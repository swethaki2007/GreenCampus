About GreenCampus

GreenCampus is a smart campus beautification and environmental engagement system created to empower university students to actively shape an eco-friendly academic environment.

🎯

Our Mission

Inspiring students to lead recycling, campus gardening, and waste reduction programs with administrative backing.

🌏

Our Vision

Creating an environmentally conscious university community where eco-friendly actions are celebrated and rewarded.

🤝

Community Impact

Fostering teamwork and leadership through active volunteering campaigns and hands-on campus improvements.
